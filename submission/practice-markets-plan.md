# Plan: 3 practice markets with a real community (Oct 8–12, 2026)

**Goal:** show judges real people using Pot in a real group, honestly labelled as practice (no real money). Get at least one settled receipt with real names on it before recording the final Loom.

**Deadline maths:** submissions close **Tue 13 Oct, 07:59 WAT**. All three markets below finish by Monday 12 Oct, so you can settle them, post receipts, record the Loom on Monday evening and submit with a night to spare. Record a backup Loom on Saturday with whatever you have by then.

## 1. Pick the group (Thu 8 Oct)
- Best: a Nigerian football or crypto Telegram group you're already in, with 30+ active members and an admin who's happy to try it. A group where people already argue about matches is ideal.
- Backup: make your own group from friends, coursemates or colleagues (aim for 10–15 people), and say so honestly.
- Add @pantapotbot to the group. It only needs to be able to post messages.
- Only group admins can run `/new` and `/settle`, so you need to be an admin of the group (or have the admin run them).

**What to tell the group (paste this):**
> Testing a bot I built for a hackathon. It turns our arguments into prediction markets. It's PRACTICE ONLY: no real money, nothing to pay, your wallet just signs a free message. If you have Phantom on your phone, tap Buy YES or Buy NO on the cards. If you don't, just reply to the card with your pick and I'll still count you in the feedback. At the end the bot posts a receipt with who was right. Takes 30 seconds.

## 2. The three markets

Check every kick-off time on premierleague.com the day before. The times below come from the Premier League's October fixture list (BST, which is the same as WAT in October).

| # | When to post | Type into the group | Buying closes | Settle | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thu 8 Oct evening | `/new Will Man United beat Spurs on Saturday 10 Oct, kick-off 5:30pm?` | Sat 10 Oct, 17:30 WAT (kick-off) | Sat night, after full time: `/settle yes` or `/settle no` | Big Nigerian fan bases on both sides, so people split, which makes a better receipt. Settles early, so you have a real receipt by Saturday night for a backup Loom. |
| 2 | Fri 9 Oct | `/new Will Liverpool beat Man City on Sunday 11 Oct, kick-off 4:30pm?` | Sun 11 Oct, 16:30 WAT (kick-off) | Sun evening, after full time | The biggest game of the weekend. Gets the most people talking. Use this one for the live-buy shot in the Loom on Saturday or Sunday morning. |
| 3 | Sat 10 Oct | `/new Will Bitcoin be above $[ROUND NUMBER] at 12:00 WAT on Monday 12 Oct?` | Pick a time a few hours before (the AI will suggest one; make sure it's before 12:00 WAT Mon) | Mon 12 Oct, just after 12:00 WAT, using the price on CoinGecko | Pulls in the crypto people, not just football fans. Pick a round number close to the price on Saturday so it's a real coin flip. Settles Monday noon, just in time for the final Loom. |

Tips:
- Post each market with a short human line before it, e.g. "Settle this argument: Man U or Spurs?" People join a fight, not a form.
- Ask one or two friends to buy early on the less popular side, so the market isn't one-sided. (One-sided markets work, but the receipt is less fun.)
- Share market 2 on X as a Blink from the card's "Share" button, and ask one person to buy from the X post. That gives you a "bought through a shared link" line.
- Ask everyone who buys from the website to DM the bot `/link`, so their Telegram name shows on the receipt instead of a wallet address.
- When you settle, use the rule's own source (premierleague.com, BBC Sport or ESPN for the matches; CoinGecko for BTC) and say which one in the group.
