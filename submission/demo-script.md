# Pot: Loom demo script (2 min 30 s target, 3 min hard stop)

**Before you hit record**
- Phone screen mirrored on the Mac (or use Telegram Desktop plus the phone for the Phantom part). Big font in Telegram.
- In the community group: one practice market already **settled** (with its receipt posted) and one **open** market with a few buys. Use the real markets from `practice-markets-plan.md`.
- Browser tabs ready, in this order: market page of the open market (`pot-navy.vercel.app/m/<id>`), its Blink preview (`/blink/<id>`), the leaderboard, the GitHub repo.
- Phantom unlocked on the phone. Notifications off.
- Shot 3 and 4 make new drafts. Do them in a small test group, or tap **Cancel** on the drafts afterwards so the community group doesn't get duplicates.
- Speak slowly. About 150 words a minute. The spoken lines are about 360 words, roughly 2.5 minutes at a steady pace.

## If you're running long
Cut shot 4's BBNaija part first, then shot 7's leaderboard. Never cut shot 8 (the receipt) or shot 9 (the honest practice-mode line).

## If something breaks while recording
- Phantom slow: it's fine to cut the wait. Say nothing; jump to the alert in the group.
- AI draft slow (it takes about 5 seconds): keep talking over it, or cut.
- Don't re-record because of one stumble. Natural is better than perfect.

## Real-people moment (strongly recommended)
If your testers agree, add 5–10 seconds of a real group member's reaction to the receipt (a reply in the chat, or a short voice note). Real people reacting is the best traction proof a practice-mode demo can have. Blur phone numbers.

## After recording
- Title the Loom: "Pot: Panta prediction markets for your group chat (demo)".
- Paste the Loom link into `description.md` and `writeup.md` where it says `[LOOM LINK HERE]`.
